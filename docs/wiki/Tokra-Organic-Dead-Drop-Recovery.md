# Tok'ra organic intelligence recovery

Starting with `0.2.49-dev`, the Tok'ra may discreetly ask a colony to secure a sealed module containing encrypted intelligence on Goa'uld activity. This is a preliminary operation and can be offered before the Tok'ra consider the colony a fully trusted contact.

## Receiving the request

A player home map needs a powered Tok'ra secure communicator. The opportunity arrives after a hidden variable delay and is not tied to a visible fixed timer.

The initial offer can be ignored without losing Tok'ra trust. To accept it:

1. select a colon capable of Intellectual work;
2. right-click the powered Tok'ra secure communicator;
3. choose **Accept Tok'ra intelligence recovery**.

Only after acceptance is the encrypted module delivered.

## Delivery location

The module uses the same delivery routing as other Tok'ra caches and medical deliveries:

1. beside or on the dedicated Tok'ra delivery drop zone when one exists;
2. beside a powered Tok'ra secure communicator only when no delivery zone exists;
3. at a reachable map edge only when neither preferred delivery target exists.

## Securing the intelligence

Select an Intellectual-capable colon, right-click the module and choose **Secure Tok'ra intelligence module**.

The colon checks the container's integrity, extracts its encrypted data and confirms transmission to the Tok'ra cell. The module contains no usable supplies and does not become an inventory item.

The data must be secured within roughly 36 in-game hours. Finishing the interaction completes the operation immediately; no second communicator transmission is required.

## Outcome

Successful recovery:

- improves Tok'ra trust by `2`;
- grants `200` Intellectual XP to the colon who secures the module;
- removes the module.

An accepted operation that expires, or whose module is destroyed or lost, reduces Tok'ra trust by `1`. Ignoring the initial offer has no penalty.

The module has no market value and provides no material reward. It cannot be hauled, minified or deconstructed, although it remains physically destructible.

## Operation variety

The scheduler can now choose between:

- discreet observation of Goa'uld activity;
- recovery of encrypted Tok'ra intelligence.

The last offered type becomes much less likely to be selected immediately again, but repetition is not impossible. This avoids a fixed or predictable alternation.
