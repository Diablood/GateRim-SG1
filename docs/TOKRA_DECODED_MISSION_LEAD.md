# 0.2.41-dev - Tok'ra decoded mission lead

This milestone turns the analyzed Tok'ra encoded intelligence into a clear operational lead without opening a full mission yet.

## Player flow

1. Request a discreet Tok'ra mission through the secure communicator.
2. Receive the automatic Tok'ra briefing.
3. Receive the mission cache containing the encoded intelligence packet.
4. Analyze the packet with an Intellectual-capable colonist.
5. Wait for the Tok'ra cell to decode the fragments and return with a limited lead.

## Result

The Tok'ra confirm a discreet clue around an isolated Goa'uld relay, probably tied to a Jaffa transit route. This is only a mission lead for a future milestone.

## Limits

This step does not create a world site, raid, reward, item delivery, healing, reinforcements, trade or recruitment.

## Status tracking

The decoded lead is stored persistently in the Tok'ra trust tracker and appears in the Tok'ra communicator status report.
