# 0.2.40-dev - Tok'ra coded intelligence analysis

## Scope

This milestone gives the Tok'ra encoded intelligence packet its first player-facing use without opening a full mission, world site or reward loop.

## Player flow

1. The colony requests a discreet Tok'ra mission through the secure communicator.
2. The Tok'ra cell sends the first briefing.
3. A small mission cache delivers an encoded intelligence packet.
4. A colon capable of Intellectual can right-click the packet and analyze it.

## Gameplay effect

- The analyzing colon works briefly at the packet.
- The colon receives a light Intellectual XP gain.
- The global Tok'ra mission state records that the coded intelligence has been analyzed.
- The Tok'ra channel status report displays the analyzed-intelligence state.

## Limits

This step deliberately does not add:

- material reward;
- direct healing;
- reinforcements;
- trade;
- recruitment;
- forced raid;
- world site;
- complete quest.

The packet is a narrative and progression hook for the future first true Tok'ra mission.
